# The Hacker's Pledge

*Read this together. Talk about each line. Sign it when you're ready.*

A **hacker** is someone who loves figuring out how things *really*
work, and who can make computers do surprising things. That's a
superpower. Superpowers come with rules.

1. **I only hack things that are mine, or things I've been invited to
   hack.** The Mineshaft, my own Raspberry Pi, my own programs: yes.
   Anything belonging to anyone else, even "just to look": no.

2. **I ask first.** If I'm not sure whether something is okay, I stop
   and ask my guide.

3. **When I find a way in, I tell the owner.** I don't use it, show it
   off, or share it around.

4. **I never use passwords that aren't mine.** In the Mineshaft, the
   game gives me the passwords. In real life, I never use someone else's.

5. **I don't break things that other people need.**

6. **I use my powers to help.** Making things, fixing things, and
   teaching other people.

Breaking into computers you don't own is against the law in most
countries, even for kids, and even if you "didn't do anything" once
you were in. Real security experts follow these exact rules. They're
paid to break in *only* when someone invites them.
